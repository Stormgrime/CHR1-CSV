CalorType Set 2 — citation snowball methodology

Input
- 240 unique seed PMIDs from For-cross-reference-set2.xlsx.

Expansion
- Generation 1: all PubMed-indexed references cited by each seed paper.
- Generation 2: all PubMed-indexed references cited by every unique generation-1 paper.
- Generation 3: selective expansion from the 2500 highest-relevance generation-2 papers.

Provenance
- citation_edges.csv.gz records parent-to-child citation links.
- seed_candidate_links.csv.gz records every seed-to-candidate association and one representative shortest path.
- citation_nodes.csv.gz contains one deduplicated row per PMID.

Ranking
- Temperature/thermal/febrile terminology.
- Mutation/variant/allele terminology.
- Protein stability, folding, trafficking, function and related mechanisms.
- Exact seed-associated gene matches.
- Support from multiple seed papers and direct-reference proximity.

Important limitations
- The graph uses NIH iCite/Open Citation Collection and therefore captures PubMed-linked references, not every bibliography entry.
- Generation 3 is relevance-gated rather than exhaustive.
- The ranked shortlist is designed for high-recall human review; evidence should be confirmed in full text.
- Forward citations are outside this build.
